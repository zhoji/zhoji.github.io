The character "development" felt so sudden, and I'm not sure why the main characters even fell for each other. Still, had heart-warming themes and was cozy. Dealt with the matter of death and grief in a gentle way.

## Synopsis

**Welcome to Charon's Crossing.
The tea is hot, the scones are fresh, and the dead are just passing through.**

When a reaper comes to collect Wallace from his own funeral, Wallace begins to suspect he might be dead.

And when Hugo, the owner of a peculiar tea shop, promises to help him cross over, Wallace decides he’s definitely dead.

But even in death he’s not ready to abandon the life he barely lived, so when Wallace is given one week to cross over, he sets about living a lifetime in seven days.

Hilarious, haunting, and kind, _Under the Whispering Door_ is an uplifting story about a life spent at the office and a death spent building a home.
